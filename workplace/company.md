# FlowCart

FlowCart is a fictional US-based B2B SaaS company headquartered in Austin, Texas.

## Product

FlowCart helps e-commerce businesses manage orders, payments, shipping, returns and post-purchase operations from one platform.

Core domains:
- Checkout
- Payments
- Orders
- Shipping
- Returns
- Merchants and multitenancy
- Analytics
- Integrations and webhooks

## Learning purpose

FlowCart provides persistent realistic workplace situations across QA, Engineering, Product, Business, DevOps and leadership.

The learner joins as a Senior QA Automation Engineer. Professional problems remain appropriate for an experienced QA professional; only the English used to communicate about them is adapted to current language ability.

## Career path

The simulated career may evolve based on demonstrated professional communication and language competence:

Senior QA Automation Engineer → QA Automation Lead → QA Lead → Quality Engineering Manager.

Progression is earned through simulation outcomes and communication competence, not automatically through CEFR advancement.
