# Roadmap — A1 to B2

## Goal

Reach conversational B2 English for international software teams, with professional independence in QA Automation, Engineering, Product, Business, interviews and leadership communication.

## A1 — Essential Professional Communication

Outcome: communicate simple professional ideas and survive basic workplace interactions.

Focus:
- introductions and career basics
- present, past and future foundations
- questions and clarification
- basic workplace vocabulary
- QA/Tech vocabulary from day one
- simple daily updates
- simple bug and ticket communication
- basic PM/Developer interaction
- interview fundamentals
- very slow to slow listening

Gate examples:
- introduce professional background orally
- answer basic interview questions
- understand simple workplace requests without transcript
- participate in a simplified daily
- ask for clarification in English

## A2 — Workplace Interaction

Outcome: participate in routine software-team conversations with growing independence.

Focus:
- responsibilities and experience
- bugs and reproduction
- tickets and requirements
- releases and deployments
- simple technical explanations
- Product fundamentals
- customer impact basics
- meetings and asynchronous communication
- structured interview answers
- slow to clear/moderate listening

Gate examples:
- explain a bug and reproduction steps
- discuss a ticket with a Developer
- understand a simple PM explanation
- maintain a short professional conversation
- complete an A2 interview simulation

## B1 — Professional Independence

Outcome: work in English in common QA/Engineering situations.

Focus:
- automation strategy
- API/UI testing
- CI/CD and architecture discussions
- risk and prioritization
- incidents and root-cause discussions
- product requirements and trade-offs
- customer/business impact
- disagreement and clarification
- Senior QA interview scenarios
- moderate to natural listening

Gate examples:
- sustain a professional discussion without systematic translation
- explain automation decisions
- participate in refinement and bug triage
- understand PM context and identify QA implications
- complete a Senior QA technical interview

## B1+ — Interview and Leadership Readiness

Internal milestone, not a separate CEFR level.

Outcome: perform credibly in international Senior QA Automation / QA Lead interviews and lead routine QA conversations.

Focus:
- STAR answers
- leadership situations
- mentoring
- conflict resolution
- quality strategy
- release recommendations
- stakeholder communication
- natural professional listening exposure

## B2 — International Professional Independence

Outcome: discuss, defend, negotiate and lead professional conversations in English.

Focus:
- QA leadership and quality strategy
- business metrics and product strategy
- stakeholder management
- technical and organizational trade-offs
- incidents and executive communication
- disagreement and negotiation
- multi-speaker meetings
- international accents
- natural speech and connected speech
- QA Lead / QA Manager interviews

B2 gate requires:
- natural-speed professional listening
- multi-speaker comprehension
- handling interruptions and ambiguity
- explaining complex technical topics
- understanding PM/business discussions
- defending QA recommendations
- completing a professional Senior/Lead interview
- functioning without systematic Spanish support

## Adaptive rule

The roadmap defines competencies, not a fixed number of lessons. The next lesson is chosen from demonstrated strengths, weaknesses, workplace needs and interview performance.
