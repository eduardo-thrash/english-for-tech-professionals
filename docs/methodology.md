# Methodology

## Learning loop

Learn → Hear → Speak → Apply → Receive feedback → Review → Reuse.

English is learned through professional communication, not as isolated grammar study.

## Session balance

A standard class lasts approximately 60 minutes:

- 30 minutes: realistic FlowCart workday simulation
- 30 minutes: targeted English development and interview practice

The workplace block is also learning time; the separation exists to ensure deliberate instruction does not replace immersion.

## Skill weighting

Speaking and listening receive the highest priority. Vocabulary is learned primarily as useful expressions and sentence patterns rather than isolated word lists. Grammar is selected based on communication needs and recurring errors.

## Contextual grammar

Grammar should answer a communication need. Example: past simple is practiced through explaining what happened during testing; modal verbs through recommendations and risk discussions.

## Listening progression

Listening uses audio first. Transcripts are delayed until after initial comprehension attempts when practical.

Progression considers:
- speech speed
- pauses
- sentence length
- contractions
- connected speech
- vocabulary
- accents
- multiple speakers
- interruptions
- professional and business complexity

## Speaking progression

Speaking prioritizes:
1. being understood
2. clarity
3. accuracy
4. fluency
5. natural delivery

The learner is not forced to speak quickly. Listening speed may progress faster than speaking speed.

## Feedback

During fluency and simulations, minor errors do not interrupt communication. After the activity, feedback prioritizes errors that:
- block meaning
- recur frequently
- are important for the current level
- reduce professional clarity
- create unnatural or ambiguous communication

## Workplace immersion

Professional complexity remains authentic. English complexity adapts to the learner.

The simulation should expose the learner to:
- Developers
- Product Managers
- Engineering Managers
- Designers
- DevOps/SRE
- Support
- executives/stakeholders

## Interviews

Interview training begins at A1 and remains present throughout the path. Basic questions are revisited at higher levels with progressively stronger answers.

## Adaptive learning

Skill dimensions progress independently. The weakest high-value skills receive increased practice. Listening and speaking weaknesses take priority when they constrain workplace communication.

## Level completion

Completing lessons does not complete a CEFR level. Advancement requires demonstrated performance through oral assessment, listening, workplace simulation and interview performance.
