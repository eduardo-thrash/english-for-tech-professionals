# Active Phrase Bank

This file is the learner's current professional speaking inventory. It is intentionally dynamic: phrases enter, move between states, and leave active rotation according to demonstrated speaking performance.

Do not treat this as a permanent vocabulary list.

## Status model

- 🔴 **Learning** — cannot yet produce reliably without modeling/support.
- 🟡 **Practicing** — can produce with a hint or in familiar context, but not yet consistently spontaneous.
- 🟢 **Active** — can produce independently and naturally in realistic contexts.
- ⚪ **Maintenance** — previously active; no longer needs frequent practice, but should be revisited occasionally.
- 🗄️ **Retired** — stable enough to remove from normal rotation. Keep only in session history, not necessarily in this active file.

## Rotation rules

At the end of every standard session:

1. Add only high-value phrases that emerged from real communication needs, corrections, interviews or workplace situations.
2. Re-evaluate existing phrases using actual speaking evidence.
3. Promote/demote phrases based on production, not lesson completion.
4. Keep the active bank compact. Target roughly 10–20 phrases in Learning + Practicing combined.
5. Move stable phrases to Active, then Maintenance.
6. Remove/retire phrases that are consistently automatic and no longer need regular retrieval.
7. Bring Maintenance phrases back temporarily if the learner starts failing to retrieve them.
8. Prefer useful sentence chunks and patterns over isolated words.
9. Reuse phrases naturally in FlowCart and interviews rather than testing them only as flashcards.
10. The most important weak phrases become Priority phrases for the next session.
