# English for Tech Professionals

A1–B2 conversational English learning system for Tech professionals, focused on QA, Software Development, Product, Business, technical interviews, and realistic workplace simulations.

## Objective

Reach conversational B2 English for international software teams, with emphasis on:

- QA and test automation
- Software development and architecture
- Product and business communication
- Technical interviews from A1 onward
- Leadership and stakeholder communication
- Listening and speaking at progressively natural speed
- Realistic workplace simulations with persistent context

## Learning Model

Each standard session lasts 60 minutes:

- 30 minutes of simulated work at FlowCart
- 30 minutes of guided English learning, listening/speaking practice, and interview training

The professional difficulty remains realistic while the English language is adapted to the learner's current level.

## Source of Truth

The repository, not conversation history, is the source of truth.

To continue the learning path:

1. Read `AGENTS.md`.
2. Read `docs/progress.md`.
3. Read `workplace/state.md`.
4. Read `interviews/progress.md`.
5. Continue from `Next session`.

## Main Commands

- `Start English session`
- `Close English session`

## Initial Step

The first session is `Session 000 — Baseline Assessment`, used to establish the learner's real starting level across speaking, listening, grammar, vocabulary, professional English, interview communication, and listening speed.
